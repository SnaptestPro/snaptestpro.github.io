Privacy Policy — SnapTest Pro

Version 2026-10-15-v1 · Last updated: 15 October 2026

Draft for review: This policy describes the app's current code paths and needs review by the app owner and qualified Indian privacy counsel before public release. It is not legal advice. The app owner must confirm the contact details, Play Console disclosures, processor contracts and backup retention settings.

SnapTest Pro is used by students and educational institutes for practice tests, exams, answer-sheet scanning and result management. This page explains what the app may collect, why it is used, who can access it and how an account can be deleted.

Information the app handles

  • Account details: name, mobile number, institute and class; an institute may add roll number, guardian name/mobile, session or profile photo.
  • Learning and exam data: test answers, scores, submitted time, progress, mistakes, scan reports and related photos or documents.
  • Institute data: test content, class/batch enrollment, attendance, assignments, requests and results that an institute admin creates or links to a student.
  • Login and technical data: password/PIN verifiers, session binding, app version, device/browser technical details and error diagnostics. Raw passwords are not intentionally stored. Depending on the authentication mode, older accounts can still use legacy SHA-256 verifiers; do not reuse an important password.
  • Device storage: the app may save settings, offline test progress, cached tests and pending sync data on the device so it can work offline.

How we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts, deliver tests and classes, score answers, show progress, operate institute features, sync offline work, send requested notifications, troubleshoot errors and respond to support or deletion requests. We do not use exam answers or student profiles to serve advertising in the current app code.

Storage and service providers

App data is stored in Google Firebase services (including Firestore and, when configured, Firebase Authentication, Storage or Cloud Messaging). The app is delivered through GitHub Pages/Firebase Hosting depending on the build. Cloudflare Worker/KV/R2 services are used for server-side authentication, notifications, files or live features when those features are enabled. Google Drive upload is an optional institute feature and is currently disabled until its OAuth client is configured. These providers process data under their own terms and infrastructure; data may be stored outside your state or country.

Who can see it

A student sees their own account and learning information. An active institute admin may see student profiles and institute exam data for that admin's institute. App owners and service providers may access data when needed to operate, secure or support the service. Publicly published leaderboards or files can be seen by people who can access the relevant app page or link. Do not upload information that the institute has no right to share.

Children and guardian permission

Students under 18 should register only with a parent or legal guardian's permission. The registration form records a confirmation of age/guardian permission with the policy version and time. The app owner and institute must obtain any additional consent or notices required for their students and intended use.

Retention and deletion

Account information is kept while the account is active or while an institute needs it to provide the service. A student can request deletion in Student Settings or through the public Delete account/data page. The deletion flow removes the student's profile and credential documents, linked test records, scan reports, mistake data, sessions and batch memberships from the app's Firestore database. It also removes a profile photo stored in that profile. Deletion cannot remove copies already exported by an institute, data held in provider backups until their normal expiry, or information a provider is independently required to retain. Ask the institute to remove its separate exports and contact app support for provider-specific questions.

Your choices

You can ask to access or correct profile information through your institute admin, turn off notifications in device settings, and request deletion using the account page. A student who cannot sign in should use the public deletion page and verify account ownership with the registered password. Do not send a password or PIN to support.

Security and incidents

The app uses access rules and server-side checks intended to limit data access. No internet service can promise perfect security. Report a suspected account or data issue through the support contact shown in the app; the owner should publish and maintain a monitored support address before release.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as the app's features, providers or legal requirements change. A new policy version is shown on this page and its version is recorded for new registrations. Contact the app owner through the current in-app Help & Support details for privacy questions or requests.
